# Escalation Guide

**Owner:** AP Operations  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-03-01

## When to escalate (ask a human)

Escalate — do not guess — when:

| Situation | Notify | Channel |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Blocked vendor | Compliance + Priya Nair | Approval/escalation note; refuse payment |
| Amount mismatch beyond tolerance | Procurement buyer for the PO | Flag; optional human question |
| Missing / no matching PO | Requesting department + AP | Ask human; do not invent a PO |
| Ambiguous vendor (no alias match) | Vendor master steward | Ask human |
| Policy conflict | Priya Nair (Finance Controls) | Pause run; durable question |

## Contacts (demo)

- **Manager / approvals:** Priya Nair — `priya.nair@acmecorp.test`
- **Operator:** `operator@acmecorp.test`
- **Compliance (blocked vendors):** flag in proof report as refused/escalated

## What to include

Every escalation or human question should include:

1. Invoice number and vendor  
2. Amount and claimed PO  
3. Policy citation (file + section)  
4. Options when asking (e.g. provide PO / reject / approve exception)

## Do not escalate

Routine successful matches under threshold, or clear policy refusals that can be completed without a decision (blocked vendor refuse), should be reported honestly without blocking other invoices.
